About SPARDA

SPARDA is a technical event organized by the IEEE Student Technical Community. It gives students a place to try ideas in technology, work through real problems, and learn by building alongside their peers.

Whatever your branch or year, the aim is the same: leave with a clearer idea of what you can build.

  • Explore technology

    Try tools, ideas, and areas of engineering that go beyond the classroom.

  • Solve problems

    Work through challenges that reward clear thinking and careful testing.

  • Build practical skills

    Turn what you have learned into something that runs and can be demonstrated.

  • Collaborate

    Meet students from other branches and see how others approach the same problem.
